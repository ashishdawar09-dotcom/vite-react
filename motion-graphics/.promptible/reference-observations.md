# Reference observations — tsenta-one-click-wall (3.05 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps)

## Composition

The reference opens so tight that the authentic job card bleeds off both frame edges: the title, the company row and the action buttons fill the width, and the "Apply" button sits in the lower-right third under the cursor. There is no empty margin or void; the light-grey world is always full of real UI. As the camera pulls back, neighbouring cards appear on all sides in a regular grid, so the frame density grows instead of the object shrinking into empty space. The end state is a dense, frame-filling wall: the scale itself is the message.

## Camera

Frames 0–10: hold tight while the cursor presses Apply; the button flips to green "Applied ✓" with a single flat ring. Frames 10–30: the cursor eases off and the camera begins a smooth pullback centred on the clicked card. Frames 30–90: continuous zoom-out with no cuts, whips or rotation, keeping the original card at the centre so cause and consequence share one coordinate system. The reference has no end hold, so our clip adds the ≥1 s editor hold the skill requires.

## Identity

Real company names and marks sit in their authentic positions inside a faithful card UI. Brand colour appears only as flat fills (pastel card tints, solid green state). Shadows are soft and neutral grey. Typography is a clean grotesk at large, readable task scale in the opening frames.

## Research match

Our authentic action is the real "05 CATEGORIES" link in the Shuttle Storm phone menu (measured 17,315 → 395.6×57.8 CSS px; real href /categories). The consequence is real and countable: the categories page lists exactly eleven rows (DOM rows 426–1078 CSS px). Like the reference, the opening frame is tight on the real link with a cursor tap and a flat press state; the payoff is a wall built from the eleven real table rows (cropped from the page, not duplicated or invented), grouped by the page's own Group column, filling the frame at readable size, with the register page's real deadline line beneath it.

## Anti-pattern

Do not imply that "Register now" opens the categories page (it opens the external registration form). Do not fake a wall by duplicating screenshots or inventing more than eleven categories. Do not invent a "Registered ✓" state. Do not place dark phones in a dark void, add glow to the volt accent, or rely on a slow zoom on a static centred phone. No captions repeating narration, no particles and no whip transitions.
