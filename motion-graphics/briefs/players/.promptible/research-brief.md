# Research brief — Shuttle Storm ’26 players

## Product or concept

Player-recruitment video for Shuttle Storm ’26. Organiser-supplied claims (user): feather shuttles will be used, and there are great prizes (no amounts or types). Site facts: '16 feathers. Set into a cork base. A tournament shuttle weighs about five grams…' (home page); the register page's 'Your day, start to finish' five steps (register online, pair up for doubles, check the draw, play your draw, semi-finals and finals); eleven categories from age 8 to 50+; entries close Nov 18.

## Authoritative sources

- https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/
- https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/register

Every surface is a real screenshot of the club's own site source in this repository (shuttlestorm-site/public), deployed at https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/, captured at 430 CSS px (3x) by scripts/capture-site.mjs, which also measures every overlay position from the live DOM into public/research/measurements.json. The mark is the site's own favicon.svg. Type is the site's own Archivo (wide, heavy for lockups, as in the site's own "Watch the storm" film end card) and JetBrains Mono.

## Real visual language

The site is near-black ink (#06080D) with bone text (#ECEEF2), a single flat volt accent (#D4FF3A), thin wide uppercase Archivo display type with italic volt accents, and mono eyebrow labels. Its own 15-second film ends on a heavy, wide SHUTTLE (bone) / STORM (volt) lockup beside the mark, with a mono line "NOV 21 2026 • UFV NORTH GYM • ABBOTSFORD BC". The user chose the clean-dark surface: neutral charcoal world (#101218), the dark site surfaces as rounded cards with a hairline border, flat volt markers only.

## Closest approved mechanism

openmontage-claude-pipeline: one connected pipeline; the camera follows causality from the hook (what you play with, what you play for) down the real five-step day into the finals and the call to register.

## Motion plan

Open with the site's own shuttle art (cropped inside the page's darker edges) filling the frame, and pull back at once as 'FEATHER SHUTTLES.' rises; the site's real '16 feathers' fact arrives once the pull-back has it whole in frame and is underlined; then 'GREAT PRIZES.' holds for ~0.5 s. One flat volt bar then draws down from the promises into the register page's real 'Your day, start to finish' (shown with ~67 px side gutters; the promises have cleared the frame by step 1), where it becomes each step's marker: each step lights as the bar's head crosses its title and the others dim, each held ~0.6 s static while the card nearly fills the frame. From step 5 the same bar runs straight on as the margin rule of the end card, with steps 3–5 still in frame above it: '11 CATEGORIES / AGES 8 TO 50+' with the site film's lockup rising alongside, then 'Entries close Nov 18' and the register URL. No empty travel; ~1.6 s end hold.

## Avoid

No invented prices, prize amounts, prize types, ranks, counts, schedules or urgency. No neon, glow, bloom, coloured shadows, gradients or glass added by the video (the site's own artwork is shown as captured). No decorative particles or whips. No re-typed site copy except headline facts that appear verbatim on the site or were supplied by the organisers. Do not recolour or redraw the Shuttle Storm mark or any sponsor logo.
