# Reference observations — openmontage-claude-pipeline

## Composition

The reference is one connected pipeline: a request terminal at the top, a drawn path leading down to each production stage, each stage a distinct real surface, ending on the output. Every frame is dense with real UI; travel bridges destinations.

## Camera

The camera follows causality down the path: hold on a stage while it works, travel along the connector, hold on the next stage, settle on the output.

## Identity

Real product names and surfaces carry identity; accents are flat (a single brand colour per state), shadows are neutral, and typography is a clean grotesk at task scale.

## Research match

openmontage-claude-pipeline: one connected pipeline; the camera follows causality from the hook (what you play with, what you play for) down the real five-step day into the finals and the call to register. The Shuttle Storm surfaces are real screenshots of the club's own pages, with every marker position measured from the DOM; headline facts are verbatim from the site or the organisers' own claims.

## Anti-pattern

Do not invent prize amounts or types, schedules, sponsor tiers or numbers; do not redraw logos; do not add glow, gradients or glass; do not let headline type collide with the site surfaces; no whip pans; no disconnected cards popping into a void — every surface lives in one world the camera travels through.
