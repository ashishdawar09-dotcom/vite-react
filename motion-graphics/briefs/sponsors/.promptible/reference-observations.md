# Reference observations — openmontage-hook

## Composition

The reference opens tight on a crowded strip of real tool tabs, pulls back to show many separate windows scattered in one light world, then the windows collapse toward one terminal at the centre with the outputs stacked beneath it as consequences.

## Camera

Tight opening, a smooth pullback to reveal the scattered world, then a hold while the pieces physically converge on the centre; the camera settles as the system becomes coherent.

## Identity

Real product names and surfaces carry identity; accents are flat (a single brand colour per state), shadows are neutral, and typography is a clean grotesk at task scale.

## Research match

openmontage-hook: fragmented pieces physically collapse into one coherent system — here, seven separate businesses' real logo tiles converge from both sides into one sponsor wall under the page's thank-you, and the consequence (the page's own 'Back the storm' ask) follows. The Shuttle Storm surfaces are real screenshots of the club's own pages, with every marker position measured from the DOM; headline facts are verbatim from the site or the organisers' own claims.

## Anti-pattern

Do not invent prize amounts or types, schedules, sponsor tiers or numbers; do not redraw logos; do not add glow, gradients or glass; do not let headline type collide with the site surfaces; no whip pans; no disconnected cards popping into a void — every surface lives in one world the camera travels through.
