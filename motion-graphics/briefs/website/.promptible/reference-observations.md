# Reference observations — kickbacks-same-run-editor

## Composition

The reference keeps the real editor surface as the whole world: code lines at readable size fill the frame edge to edge, with a status row at the bottom. There is no decorative card floating in a void; detail stays attached to its working context.

## Camera

Restrained: the camera eases between the line being edited and the cost/status readout, with holds while the state changes. No whips; movement exists only to follow the action.

## Identity

Real product names and surfaces carry identity; accents are flat (a single brand colour per state), shadows are neutral, and typography is a clean grotesk at task scale.

## Research match

kickbacks-same-run-editor: the real site pages are the working surfaces; the camera travels page to page in one horizontal world and the marker action happens on the page itself. The Shuttle Storm surfaces are real screenshots of the club's own pages, with every marker position measured from the DOM; headline facts are verbatim from the site or the organisers' own claims.

## Anti-pattern

Do not invent prize amounts or types, schedules, sponsor tiers or numbers; do not redraw logos; do not add glow, gradients or glass; do not let headline type collide with the site surfaces; no whip pans; no disconnected cards popping into a void — every surface lives in one world the camera travels through.
