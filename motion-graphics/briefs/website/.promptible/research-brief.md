# Research brief — Shuttle Storm ’26 website

## Product or concept

A 16:9, 15-second film sized for the home page's existing 'The film — Watch the storm. Fifteen seconds of speed, precision and Shuttle Storm' section. Facts from the site plus the organisers' two claims (feather shuttles, great prizes).

## Authoritative sources

- https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/
- https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/categories
- https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/register

Every surface is a real screenshot of the club's own site source in this repository (shuttlestorm-site/public), deployed at https://shuttlestorm.adawar.org/, captured at 430 CSS px (3x) by scripts/capture-site.mjs, which also measures every overlay position from the live DOM into public/research/measurements.json. The mark is the site's own favicon.svg. Type is the site's own Archivo (wide, heavy for lockups, as in the site's own "Watch the storm" film end card) and JetBrains Mono.

## Real visual language

The site is near-black ink (#06080D) with bone text (#ECEEF2), a single flat volt accent (#D4FF3A), thin wide uppercase Archivo display type with italic volt accents, and mono eyebrow labels. Its own 15-second film ends on a heavy, wide SHUTTLE (bone) / STORM (volt) lockup beside the mark, with a mono line "NOV 21 2026 • UFV NORTH GYM • ABBOTSFORD BC". The user chose the clean-dark surface: neutral charcoal world (#101218), the dark site surfaces as rounded cards with a hairline border, flat volt markers only.

## Closest approved mechanism

kickbacks-same-run-editor: the real site pages are the working surfaces; the camera travels page to page in one horizontal world and the marker action happens on the page itself.

## Motion plan

Four stops in one horizontal world, each a real site page beside its fact in the site's display type: home hero (date, ages underlined) with 'SAT · NOV 21 / UFV NORTH GYM'; the categories table (volt marks down all eleven rows) with '11 CATEGORIES / JUNIORS TO MASTERS.'; the home facts ('16 feathers' underlined) with 'FEATHER SHUTTLES. GREAT PRIZES.'; the register page (the 'Entries close…' line underlined) with '$30–40 / ENTRIES CLOSE NOV 18'. Each stop holds ~2.4 s; travel is a 24-frame pan with a shallow pull-back. Ends on the site film's own end card and address, ~2.8 s hold.

## Avoid

No invented prices, prize amounts, prize types, ranks, counts, schedules or urgency. No neon, glow, bloom, coloured shadows, gradients or glass added by the video (the site's own artwork is shown as captured). No decorative particles or whips. No re-typed site copy except headline facts that appear verbatim on the site or were supplied by the organisers. Do not recolour or redraw the Shuttle Storm mark or any sponsor logo.
