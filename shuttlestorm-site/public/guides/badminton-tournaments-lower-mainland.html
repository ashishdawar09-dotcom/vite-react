Types of badminton tournaments in BC

  • Junior series (A, B and C). Badminton BC’s junior circuits, from the C series up to the top-level A series. Junior B results count toward qualifying for the BC Junior B Provincials.
  • Open series. Adult events split into skill levels, such as Open A and Open B.
  • Masters series. Age-group events for players 30 and over.
  • Collegiate series. Events for college and university players.
  • Club and community opens. Tournaments run by local clubs, like Shuttle Storm ’26. They make a good first tournament: you enter by age group, and everyone in an event with three or more entries plays at least two matches.

How to find and enter a tournament

  1. Check the Badminton BC event calendar. Badminton BC says registration links usually appear about four to five weeks before each event.
  2. Open the event page and read the eligibility rules, categories and entry deadline.
  3. Find a doubles partner early. Most events close entries several days before play.
  4. Register and pay online, then watch for the draw, which is usually posted a day or two before play.

What a tournament day looks like

Most tournaments follow the same rhythm, whether it is a Badminton BC series event or a club open:

  • The draw comes first. Organisers post draws and start times shortly before the event. Small events often play a round robin, so everyone meets everyone; larger ones use an elimination draw.
  • Matches are called, not scheduled to the minute. Times move with the results. Stay near the court and listen for your name: at Shuttle Storm, a player who is not on court within 10 minutes of being called loses by default.
  • Scoring is rally scoring. Every rally wins a point, under the BWF Laws of Badminton. Some events shorten matches to fit the day; Shuttle Storm plays single games to 21 through the quarter-finals, then best of three games to 15.
  • You won’t play all day. Expect gaps between matches. Bring water, food and a warm layer, and warm up again before each match.
  • Gear. Non-marking indoor court shoes are required at most venues. Protective eyewear is a good idea, especially in doubles.

Frequently asked questions

Are there badminton tournaments in the Lower Mainland this fall?

Yes. Badminton BC lists sanctioned events in Vancouver, Port Moody and Richmond in November and December 2026, and Shuttle Storm ’26 runs in Abbotsford on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Check the Badminton BC event calendar for the full, current list.

When do entries open for Badminton BC tournaments?

Badminton BC says registration links usually appear on its event calendar about four to five weeks before each event.

Is there a badminton tournament in Abbotsford?

Yes. Shuttle Storm ’26 is on Saturday, November 21, 2026 at the UFV Athletic Centre North Gym in Abbotsford, with ten categories for ages 13 to 50+. Entries close Wednesday, November 18 at 11:59 pm.

Can I enter a tournament if I have never played one?

Club opens like Shuttle Storm are open to all badminton players and group events by age. For sanctioned series events, read the eligibility rules on each event page before entering.
