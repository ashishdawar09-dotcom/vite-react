Acknowledgement of Risk and Release of Liability

You accept this waiver online when you register. Players under 19 need a parent or guardian to accept for them. Doubles partners receive an email link to accept it.

I agree to accept these Risks and agree to the terms of this waiver EVEN IF the Host Organizing Committee are found to be negligent or in breach of any duty or care or any obligation to me in my participation in the Activities involved.
  3. I confirm that I have reached the age of majority in the Province of British Columbia or my parents have read this document and allowed me to participate.
  4. I willingly agree to comply with the stated and customary terms and conditions for participation. If, however, I observe any unusual significant hazard during my presence or participation, I will remove myself from participation and bring such to the attention of the nearest official immediately.
  5. In addition to consideration given to the Host Organizing Committee for my participation in the tournament, I and my heirs, next of kin, executors, administrators and assigns (collectively my “Legal Representatives”), agree:
    • To waive all claims that I have or may have in the future against the Host Organizing Committee;

I hereby irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Province of British Columbia. Any litigation to enforce this waiver must be instituted in the Province of British Columbia.
