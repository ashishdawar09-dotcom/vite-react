Entry fees

  • Age 18+: $40 per player, per category
  • UFV students: $30 per player, per category (student ID may be checked at check-in)
  • Youth (under 18 on November 21, 2026): $30 per player, per category

Pay online when you enter, by Interac e-Transfer or by card (3% more). Entries without payment aren’t accepted. In doubles the fee is per player, at each player’s own rate: one partner can pay for both, or each pays their own.

Your day, start to finish

  1. Register online. Pick your category, accept the rules and waiver and pay online. Players under 19 need a parent or guardian to accept for them.
  2. Pair up for doubles. One partner registers the team. We email the other partner a link to accept the waiver. Enter both players by the deadline, or we’ll pair you with another player if one is available.
  3. Check the draw. Partner pairings are posted by November 19, and draws and start times by November 20. Arrive at least 30 minutes before your match.
  4. Play your draw. Events with 3 or 4 entries play round robin; events with 5 or more play an elimination draw. Early rounds are a single game to 21.
  5. Semi-finals and finals. Best of three games to 15 points, for the title in each category.

Key dates

  • Entry opens: October 10, 2026
  • Entry and withdrawal deadline: 11:59 pm, November 18, 2026
  • Partner pairings posted: by November 19
  • Draws and start times posted: by November 20
  • Tournament: Saturday, November 21, 2026

Refunds

Withdraw before entries close on November 18 for a full refund. After that and before the draws, you’re refunded less a $15 service charge. Medical withdrawals with a signed physician’s note are refunded in full. Refunds are processed after the event and take about two weeks. Full refund policy
