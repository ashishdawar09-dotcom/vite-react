Schedule (tentative)

  • Date: Saturday, November 21, 2026

You don’t need to be there all day. Arrive at least 30 minutes before your first match: if you’re not on court within 10 minutes of your match being called, it’s a default.

Parking

A special full-day parking rate of $5 is being arranged; we’ll share the link or QR code before the event. Free street parking is also available along McKenzie Road.

What to wear and bring

  • Athletic clothing and non-marking indoor court shoes.
  • Protective eyewear meeting ASTM F803 is highly recommended on court.
  • Cell phone calls are not permitted in the badminton hall.

On site

  • A first aid attendant will be available.
  • Emergency action and evacuation plans are posted at the venue.
