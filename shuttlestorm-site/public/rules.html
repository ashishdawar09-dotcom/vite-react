Entry deadlines

  • Entry opens: October 10, 2026
  • Entry closes: 11:59 pm, November 18, 2026
  • Withdrawal deadline: 11:59 pm, November 18, 2026

There will be a limit on the maximum number of entries. Enter early and don’t be disappointed.

Entry is online only, through the tournament registration page. No manual entries will be accepted.

  • Any age group with 3 or fewer entries will be combined if possible.
  • Changes to an entry are not permitted after the entry deadline.
  • In doubles and mixed doubles, both players must be entered by the entry deadline, or they will be partnered with another player if available.
  • Partner requests, changes and pairings are accepted until the entry close date; after that, no changes are allowed.
  • Partner pairings will be posted by November 19 for review. Questions or clarifications must be raised with the HOC before the draw is published.

To avoid disappointment, don’t leave your registration to the last moment in case you need help with it.

When you enter, you accept these regulations and the waiver online and sign by typing your name. Players under 19 need a parent or guardian to accept for them. In doubles, your partner receives an email link to accept the waiver themselves.

Entry fees

  • Age 18+: $40
  • UFV students: $30 (student ID may be checked at check-in)
  • Youth (under age 18): $30

Payment is made online at the same time as entry. Entries not completed online, and entries without payment, will not be accepted. Card payments cost 3% more than Interac e-Transfer. In doubles the fee is per player, at each player’s own rate.

Age groups

Ages are determined as of the first day of the tournament, November 21, 2026. Open to all badminton players. See the event categories.

A doubles pair’s age group is set by the younger partner, so both players must be 35 or older to enter a 35+ event. 35+ has no upper age limit: players over 50 can enter it as well as Super Doubles 50+. Anyone 18 or older can enter an 18–34 event. Players under 13 may still enter by playing up into Youth Singles.

Tournament format

The draw format will be adapted to the number of entries.

  • Events with 5 or more entries: elimination draw.
  • Events with 3 or 4 entries: round robin.
  • Players are guaranteed a minimum of 2 matches per event with 3 or more entries.

Scoring. Round robin matches and matches up to the quarter-final stage are a single game of 21 points. Semi-finals and finals are best of 3 games of 15 points each.

Schedule (tentative)

9:30 am to 5 pm. Times are subject to change according to the number of entries in each event. Draws and start times will be posted by November 20, 2026.

Parking

A special full-day parking rate of $5 is being arranged. We will share the link or QR code to get the special rate. Free street parking is available along McKenzie Road.

Event medical services

  • Emergency action plan: posted at the venue
  • Emergency evacuation plan: posted at the venue
  • First aid attendant: a first aid person will be available

Venue regulations

  • Appropriate athletic attire is required on court. Shorts that extend below the knee are deemed inappropriate clothing.
  • Non-marking indoor court shoes only.
  • Officials reserve the right to ask competitors to change inappropriate clothing. The Tournament Referee makes the final decision.
  • Cell phone calls are not permitted in the badminton hall.

Withdrawals and refunds

  1. Withdrawal before the entry close date will receive a full refund.
  2. Withdrawal after the entry close date but before the draws are done will receive a refund less a $15 service charge.
  3. Withdrawal due to a medical issue will receive a full refund, provided a signed physician’s note is received by the staff one day before the first day of the competition.
  4. Withdrawal from an event due to a partner’s medical illness will receive a refund for that event, provided the player is not reassigned to, or does not request, another partner already registered for the tournament.
  5. All refunds will be processed after the event has finished. Processing takes approximately two weeks.

Player responsibilities

  1. Players are recommended to be at the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match.
  2. If players are not on court within 10 minutes of their match being announced, the match is defaulted and deemed a “No Show”.
  3. Players have a 3-minute warm-up period.
  4. No additional warm-up time will be allotted for players arriving late to the court within the 10-minute default.

Eyewear

All players actively participating in badminton are highly recommended to wear protective eyewear meeting the ASTM F803 standard while on court, including goggles worn over prescription glasses. This applies during warm-up, competition and match play.

Photos

The organizers, their representatives and successors reserve all rights to reproduce, for illustration, fundraising, advertising or publication in any manner, any photographs taken during this event.

Technical officials

Technical officials will perform their duties as outlined in the Badminton BC Technical Officials Code of Conduct, the Badminton Canada Code of Conduct and the BWF regulations.
