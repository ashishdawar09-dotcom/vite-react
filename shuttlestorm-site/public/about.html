Shuttle Storm is a badminton club based in Abbotsford, in the Fraser Valley. We organise tournaments across the Lower Mainland for juniors, youth and adults who want more than drop-in play.

Every Shuttle Storm event is run at a provincial level: proper age and event categories, rally scoring, fair draws and schedules that keep you on court instead of waiting around. Whether it’s your first tournament or your fiftieth, you’ll get real matches against players who push you.

What we stand for

  • Fraser Valley roots. A local club, built by players.
  • Lower Mainland events. Tournaments across the region.
  • Provincial-level play. Competitive draws, run properly.
