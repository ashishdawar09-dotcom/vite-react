About Shuttle Storm Badminton ClubBuilt for competition.

Shuttle Storm is a badminton club based in the Fraser Valley. We organise tournaments across the Lower Mainland for juniors, youth and adults who want more than drop-in play.

Every Shuttle Storm event is run at a provincial level: proper age and event categories, rally scoring, fair draws and schedules that keep you on court instead of waiting around. Whether it’s your first tournament or your fiftieth, you’ll get real matches against players who push you.

Format and scoringEvery point.
Counts.

Rally scoring under the BWF Laws of Badminton, with a shortened format: single games to 21 through the quarter-finals. Semi-finals and finals are best of three games to 15. Everyone in an event with three or more entries plays at least two matches.

Tournament FAQGood to know.

Do I need to be there all day?
No. Draws and start times are posted by November 20. Arrive at least 30 minutes before your first match: if you’re not on court within 10 minutes of your match being called, it’s a default.
How many games will I play?
Events with 3 or 4 entries play round robin, and events with 5 or more play an elimination draw. Everyone in an event with three or more entries is guaranteed at least two matches. Matches through the quarter-finals are one game to 21; semi-finals and finals are best of three games to 15.
How does doubles registration work?
One partner registers the team and enters their partner’s details. The fee is per player, so pay for both or each pay your own. Both players must be entered by November 18. No partner? No problem. Enter solo and we’ll find you a partner. Pairings are posted by November 19.
What shuttles are used?
Tournament-grade feather shuttles, on every court and in every match.
Are there prizes?
Yes. There are prizes for the winner and runner-up in every category.
Is Shuttle Storm registered with Badminton BC?
Yes. Shuttle Storm Badminton Club is a registered Badminton BC club.
Can I enter more than one category?
Yes. The entry fee applies to each category you enter. We schedule to avoid clashes between your events wherever we can.
What if I can’t make it?
Withdraw before entries close on November 18 for a full refund. After that and before the draws, you’re refunded less a $15 service charge. Medical withdrawals with a signed physician’s note are refunded in full. Refunds are processed after the event and take about two weeks. Full refund policy
Where do I park?
A $5 all-day parking rate is being arranged; we’ll share the link or QR code before the event. Free street parking is also available along McKenzie Road.
What should I wear?
Athletic clothing and non-marking indoor court shoes. Protective eyewear meeting ASTM F803 is highly recommended on court.
Where are the full rules and the waiver?
Read the competition regulations and the waiver and release of liability. You accept them when you register; for players under 19, a parent or guardian accepts. Your doubles partner gets an email link to accept too.
