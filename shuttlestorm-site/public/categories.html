UFV students enter adult categories for $30. You can enter more than one category: the fee applies to each one, and we schedule to avoid clashes between your events wherever we can. Any age group with 3 or fewer entries will be combined if possible.

Age groups

  • Your age is your age on November 21, 2026, the day of the tournament.
  • In doubles, the younger partner sets the pair’s age group. Both players must be 35 or older to enter a 35+ event.
  • 35+ has no upper age limit. Players over 50 can enter a 35+ event as well as Super Doubles 50+.
  • Anyone 18 or older can enter an 18–34 event.
  • Players under 13 may still enter by playing up into Youth Singles. A parent or guardian must complete their registration.

Format

  • Events with 3 or 4 entries: round robin.
  • Events with 5 or more entries: elimination draw.
  • Everyone in an event with three or more entries plays at least two matches.

Scoring

Round robin matches and matches up to the quarter-finals are a single game to 21. Semi-finals and finals are best of three games to 15.

Doubles

One partner registers the team and enters their partner’s details; we email the partner a link to accept the waiver. Both players must be entered by November 18. No partner? No problem. Enter solo and we’ll find you a partner. Partner pairings are posted by November 19. How to register
